01, The Craft

The work, laid out.

A small collection of work from my early book. Extensions and color are where most of my heart goes, custom nail art is my longest-standing craft, and cutting and tinsel sit alongside. More gets added as the book grows.

Extension methods

Tape-ins

Quick to install and light to wear, made for blending short layers and adding everyday density.

K-tips

Heat-fusion bonds with near-invisible attachment, water-resistant for people who don't slow down for their hair.

I-tips

Bead-and-strand installation, full movement, reusable hair, and a longer wear cycle for the long arc.

Wefts

Sewn onto a beaded foundation. The fullest coverage and the most dramatic shift in length and density.

Tinsel

Sparkle strands woven into your existing hair, low commitment and surprising joy, the easiest way to try something playful without the install of full extensions.

Color services

Vivids

Bold fashion color and dimensional fantasy palettes. The kind of work where natural isn't the goal and the whole point is intention.

Blonding

Custom designed blonding, fit for every lifestyle and client, from bottle blondes and platinum to bleach retouches, dimensional blondes, and balayage. Real formulation behind every direction.

Color formulation

Single-process classics, custom formulation for your specific hair, glosses, and corrective work when you need to undo what came before. Where the chemistry actually shows.

Cutting

Women's cuts

Precision cutting built around how you actually wear your hair, from signature blunts and long layers to soft shapes that hold their form between visits.

Men's cuts

Sharp men's cutting, scissor work, fades, and beard shaping where the precision shows.

Nails

Nail art is where I have the most years of experience behind me. Complex hand-painted designs, themed sets, intricate detail work, the kind of nails that become a real piece of small art on the hand. The technical services below are the foundation, the art is what I love.

Custom nail art

Hand-painted designs, themed and seasonal sets, intricate detail and color work. The level of complexity scales to whatever you bring me. This is my deepest specialty.

Acrylic

Classic acrylic sets with custom shaping, length, and color, built for daily life and longer wear.

Gel-X

Soft gel tip extensions, lighter than acrylic, gentler on the natural nail underneath, cured fast and worn long.

Gel overlay

Gel cured directly over your natural nail for a strong, glossy finish without an extension. Strength without commitment.

What's right for you depends on your hair, your lifestyle, and your goals. We'll work it out together in the consult, and I'll tell you honestly even if the answer isn't what you came in expecting.

03

How the care comes back.

Care, returning
An invitation

I want to grow as a service provider through a community of people who already know me, who tell the people they love, so when you send someone my way I want to send something real back to you in return. Rather than ask you to chase a punchcard or watch a leaderboard, every dollar you spend with me adds to your points, and every person you bring through my door adds many more of them, because the trust it takes to send me a friend matters far more to me than any single appointment ever will.

The points don't expire and they don't put you in competition with anyone, they're just my way of giving back to those who trust me so that when you come back I can make sure the care is moving in both directions.

What your points come back as
  • Take-home care kits between visits
  • Discounts on services
  • Treatments such as K18, gloss, or deep conditioning
  • Complimentary service add-ons
  • Complimentary maintenance installs
  • and more to come

When we meet for your consultation, just tell me who referred you and I'll take the rest from there.

04

The why.

Being truly seen and valued is what makes people whole, and that can happen at a campfire, over a hot meal, or in a chair. My work is to build that moment into every service I do, every system I touch, and every space I create, because skill without care is empty and care without skill fails people, and I refuse to choose between them.

Jesus would wash their feet, so I will wash their hair.

Part of this work also happens outside the chair, through community days at my church and local schools, where clean and dignified hair is treated like the right it should be rather than a luxury.

05

Hair, considered.

Extensions are part of a longer relationship with your hair, not a single one-time event.

The way they're installed matters, the way they're cared for between visits matters, and the bond and scalp underneath them matter most of all. Treatments like K18, careful removal, and ongoing maintenance aren't extras I sell on top of an install, they're how the work actually holds up over time and how the hair underneath stays healthy.

Healthy in, healthy out, healthy underneath. That's the standard, not the upgrade.

Where I'm trained.

My foundation is being built at The Avenue Academy in Albuquerque, where I'm finishing cosmetology school and graduating in December 2026. In April 2026 I completed Bellami's Professional Extension Masterclass, which certified me in the four core methods I work in: tape-ins, K-tips, I-tips, and wefts.

Beyond formal training, most of what shapes how I work comes from shadowing the stylists I trust, from constant reading on hair health and color science, and from treating every install I do like the most important one I've done so far.
